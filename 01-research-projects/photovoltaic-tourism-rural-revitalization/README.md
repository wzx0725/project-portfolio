# Photovoltaic + Tourism: Rural Revitalization

**The Value Realization Mechanism and Empirical Study of Photovoltaic-Tourism Integration: A Case Study of Xuzhou, China**

光伏 + 旅游赋能乡村振兴的价值实现机理与实证研究——以徐州市为例

## 1. Project Overview

This research investigates how photovoltaic (PV) development can be integrated with rural tourism to promote rural revitalization and achieve synergistic economic, ecological, and social benefits.

Taking Xuzhou, a traditional coal-based resource city in China, as the case study, the project explores how renewable energy infrastructure can generate value beyond electricity production through industrial integration, ecological value realization, and rural income generation.

本研究以徐州市为案例，探讨光伏产业与乡村旅游融合发展的价值实现机制，分析能源转型如何与生态价值转化、乡村产业发展及农民增收形成协同效应。

## 2. Research Questions

- How can photovoltaic development contribute to rural economic transformation?
- Through what mechanisms can PV infrastructure generate tourism and ecological value?
- What are the key factors influencing the relationship between PV development and rural residents' income?
- What constraints emerge when photovoltaic development relies primarily on capacity expansion?

## 3. Analytical Framework

The research examines value creation across three dimensions:

| Dimension | Research Focus |
|---|---|
| Economic Value | Industrial integration, tourism development and landscape value |
| Ecological Value | Clean energy substitution and carbon emission reduction |
| Social Value | Employment opportunities, income generation and benefit-sharing mechanisms |

## 4. Methodology

The project combines conventional quantitative analysis with machine learning and explainable AI.

| Method | Application |
|---|---|
| Entropy Weight Method | Objective weighting of evaluation indicators |
| Contingent Valuation Method (CVM) | Estimation of willingness to pay for photovoltaic landscape value |
| Panel Data Regression | Analysis of regional economic, energy and social relationships |
| XGBoost | Modeling nonlinear relationships and identifying important influencing factors |
| SHAP / Explainable AI | Interpreting model predictions and examining feature contributions |

## 5. Key Research Findings

- Identified photovoltaic installed capacity as an important indicator in the rural revitalization evaluation framework.
- Explored the potential of landscape value transmission in connecting photovoltaic infrastructure with rural tourism.
- Examined the ecological and economic benefits of photovoltaic development.
- Identified nonlinear effects and potential income-growth constraints associated with photovoltaic capacity expansion through XGBoost and SHAP analysis.
- Explored integrated development pathways connecting energy transition, ecological restoration and rural tourism.

## 7. Research Limitations

The study identifies several areas requiring further investigation:

- More extensive local willingness-to-pay surveys are needed to improve CVM estimation.
- Longer-term data are needed to assess the sustainability of photovoltaic-tourism integration.
- Comparative analysis of different integration models requires further refinement.
- Micro-level research on farmers, tourists and enterprises could provide deeper insights into stakeholder behavior.

## 8. Future Directions

- Improve the local empirical basis for photovoltaic landscape valuation.
- Explore more refined economic evaluation of different PV-tourism integration models.
- Strengthen dynamic analysis of long-term economic and ecological effects.
- Investigate the application of interpretable machine learning in regional energy and environmental studies.
