# Entropy Weight Method (EWM)

## 1. Research Objective

The Entropy Weight Method was employed to determine the objective weights of evaluation indicators in the comprehensive assessment of rural revitalization in Xuzhou, China.

The purpose was to quantify the relative information provided by different economic and social indicators and identify the indicators exhibiting the greatest variation within the evaluation system.

本研究采用熵权法对徐州市乡村振兴综合评价指标进行客观赋权，以识别不同经济社会指标在评价体系中的相对信息差异。

## 2. Indicator System and Data

The evaluation system incorporates five secondary indicators:

| Code | Indicator | Description |
|---|---|---|
| X1 | Regional GDP | Overall regional economic development |
| X2 | Rural Residents' Per Capita Disposable Income | Rural household economic conditions |
| X3 | Primary Industry Value Added | Agricultural economic development |
| X4 | Estimated Photovoltaic Installed Capacity | Photovoltaic industry development |
| X5 | Industrial Value Added | Industrial economic development |

**Study period:** 2015–2023

**Study area:** Xuzhou, Jiangsu Province, China

The indicators involve different measurement units, including monetary values, income and installed capacity. Therefore, normalization was conducted before calculating indicator weights.

## 3. Methodological Procedure

### Step 1. Data Normalization

The range normalization method (Min-Max Normalization) was applied to eliminate differences in measurement scales.

The original indicator values were transformed into dimensionless standardized values.

### Step 2. Calculation of Indicator Proportions

The proportion of each evaluation object under each indicator was calculated using the normalized data.

This step provides the basis for measuring the information entropy of each indicator.

### Step 3. Information Entropy Calculation

The information entropy of each indicator was calculated to measure the degree of information dispersion across the evaluation objects.

The normalization constant was defined as:

k = 1 / ln(m)

where:

- m represents the total number of evaluation objects.
- k represents the normalization constant.
- e_j represents the information entropy of indicator j.

### Step 4. Difference Coefficient Calculation

The difference coefficient was derived from the information entropy of each indicator.

Indicators with greater dispersion contain more distinguishing information within the evaluation system.

### Step 5. Objective Weight Determination

The objective weight of each indicator was determined according to its difference coefficient relative to the total difference coefficients.

The resulting weights were used in the comprehensive evaluation framework.

## 4. Empirical Results

The calculated indicator weights are presented below:

| Rank | Indicator | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | X4: Estimated PV Installed Capacity | 0.2274 |
| 2 | X5: Industrial Value Added | 0.2135 |
| 3 | X1: Regional GDP | 0.1899 |
| 4 | X2: Rural Residents' Per Capita Disposable Income | 0.1893 |
| 5 | X3: Primary Industry Value Added | 0.1799 |

**Key finding:**

Photovoltaic installed capacity (X4) received the highest weight of 0.2274, followed by industrial value added (X5) at 0.2135.

This indicates that photovoltaic installed capacity exhibited the greatest relative information contribution, as measured by the entropy-weighting procedure, among the five indicators.

## 5. Research Interpretation

The results highlight the importance of photovoltaic industry development within the constructed rural revitalization evaluation framework.

The relatively high weight assigned to photovoltaic installed capacity suggests that its variation was particularly informative in distinguishing the evaluation observations.

Industrial value added ranked second, indicating the importance of the industrial development dimension in the evaluation system.

## 6. Methodological Limitations and Considerations

- Entropy weights depend on the variation and distribution of the observed data.
- The resulting weights are sensitive to indicator selection, sample coverage and data normalization.
- A higher entropy weight does not establish a causal relationship between an indicator and rural revitalization.
- Estimated photovoltaic installed capacity introduces uncertainty that should be considered when interpreting the results.
- The evaluation results should be interpreted alongside econometric analysis and other empirical evidence.

## 7. Personal Reflection

This method provided an objective weighting approach for integrating indicators measured in different units.

An important methodological insight is the distinction between statistical information contribution and causal influence. Although photovoltaic installed capacity received the highest entropy weight, further empirical analysis is necessary to investigate whether and how photovoltaic development affects rural income and regional economic transformation.
